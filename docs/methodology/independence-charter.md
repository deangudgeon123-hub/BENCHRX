# BENCHRX independence charter

Version: `independence-charter-v1`  
Prepared: 2026-10-21  
Status: initial proposed governing policy; organisational adoption and external
oversight are pending. This document is not evidence of accreditation or a
completed independent review.

## Commercial independence

Customers pay for the execution, evidence collection and reporting of an
evaluation, never for a favourable score, a passed test or a verification badge.
Sales staff, investors, customers and commercial partners must not alter a
conclusion or select scoring rules after seeing the results. Disclose relevant
funding, paid relationships and reviewer conflicts alongside the scope of any
published conclusion, subject to lawful privacy requirements.

Scoring and evaluation contracts are fixed and versioned before a campaign.
Changes require a recorded rationale, regression validation, effective version
and approval record. Compare versions explicitly; do not silently rewrite
historical scores, questions, weights or coverage requirements. Commercial
requests for new tests may inform a future documented suite, not a private rule
change that makes one customer's existing evaluation look better.

## Complete and traceable reporting

Record the intended campaign scope, agents/configurations, suite/evaluator/policy
versions, inclusion rules, stopping rules and planned repeats before execution.
Retain failed, inconclusive, infrastructure-error and unavailable-evidence
outcomes. An agent's claimed action is not proof of a state change.

Do not selectively conceal failed tests or failed runs to manufacture a favourable
public outcome. Confidential evaluation may remain private as agreed; any public
excerpt must identify its limited scope and must not imply it represents the
whole programme. An inability to publish sensitive evidence must appear as a
limitation, not be turned into a pass. Report changes in selection scope and
unexecuted tests explicitly. The current offline reporting tool cannot prove
campaign completeness; a controlled campaign register remains to be implemented.

Material corrections produce new, linked revisions with date, author, reason,
old/new methodology versions where relevant and affected conclusions. Retain
previous artifacts as permitted and notify affected report recipients through
an approved process. Privacy-driven deletion must leave an appropriate correction
record without retaining prohibited personal data or secrets.

## Evidence and claims

Conclusions must be supported by permitted recorded evidence and disclosed
contracts. Missing evidence cannot establish success. Distinguish behavioural
failure, uncertainty, infrastructure failure and evidence unavailability.
Do not equate basic conversational compliance with verified operational success
or universal production readiness.

An internal review is performed by the development team or its automated tests.
An independent external review requires real, appropriately qualified people
whose identities, relevant relationships, prior exposure and conflicts have been
checked, with access sufficient to question and reproduce the stated conclusions.
Formal accreditation requires an actual grant by an appropriate accreditation
body under a named scheme. These three statuses must never be conflated.

No independent validation, external expert participation, accuracy estimate,
reviewer identity or accreditation may be invented. The current programme is
internally implemented tooling with external validation PENDING. No independently
verified badge is authorised. No evidence currently supports a broad claim that
BENCHRX conclusions have independently assessed evaluation criteria.

## Challenge, confidentiality and governance

External researchers and security experts must be able to challenge methodology,
submit contradictory cases and request correction. Log the challenged version,
evidence, handling decision and reason. Publish material methodological changes
and sanitised decisions when permitted. Do not make access contingent on a
favourable review or suppress a sound criticism because it affects revenue.

Agree evidence access, retention, publication and confidentiality before a review.
Use pseudonymous reviewer IDs where appropriate, with identity verified privately
by the coordinator. Use synthetic secrets and isolated customer data for testing.
Do not expose real credentials, customer records or personal metadata in public
repositories. Confidentiality must not be used to make misleading public claims.

The owner must appoint an accountable methodology steward and a separate review
coordinator where feasible, record adoption of this charter and maintain conflict,
campaign, review and correction registers. These are governance commitments to
establish, not claims that those officers or registers already exist. Periodic
review must assess both technical validity and commercial independence.
