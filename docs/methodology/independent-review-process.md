# Independent technical review process v1

Status: prepared process, external participants and completed reviews PENDING.
No reviewers have been recruited, contacted, impersonated or generated by the
software implementation. No accreditation has been sought or obtained here.

1. Appoint a coordinator and define a review question, intended claim and scope.
   Publish or escrow the protocol and dataset/evaluator commitments before
   unblinding. Define acceptance criteria, statistical method, sampling design,
   repeat/group treatment and handling of disagreements in advance. This initial
   implementation intentionally does not invent an accuracy threshold.
2. Recruit at least two genuinely independent human reviewers with relevant AI
   evaluation, statistical or security expertise for the scope. Verify identity
   and qualifications privately. Record date, stable ID, affiliations, payment,
   financial/technical relationships, involvement in evaluator development and
   prior exposure to the cases or reference labels. Fixed compensation must not
   depend on agreement or a favourable conclusion. Resolve or disclose conflicts;
   do not call a conflicted review independent merely because a form says true.
3. Supply the blinded packet and contract instructions only. Keep labels,
   BENCHRX predictions, developer commentary and other reviews unavailable until
   submissions close. Track packet hash, distribution date and acknowledgements.
   The initial 30-case set is already a development set, so independent labelling
   of it does not establish held-out generalisation.
4. Collect complete verdicts and rationales separately, preserving original files
   and hashes. Import with the external-review tool. Report every review, pairwise
   agreement, confusion matrices, unresolved cases, limitations and conflicts.
   Do not treat human agreement as infallible ground truth or pool correlated
   repeated labels as independent samples.
5. Preserve disagreements. If needed, use a third qualified independent adjudicator
   who records reasoning, referenced original submissions and whether the contract
   itself is ambiguous. Keep adjudication separate and immutable; never overwrite
   labels simply to agree with BENCHRX. A discovered defect becomes a versioned
   change request, not a retrospective edit to the acceptance evidence.
6. Expand to externally supplied, permitted real agent observations and a custodian
   controlled held-out set across multiple agent types and contract families.
   Keep related tasks/paraphrases together, freeze the evaluator before release
   and retire unblinded examples from future claims of untouched validation.
7. Give reviewers exported evidence and the matching source/dependency environment
   to reproduce permitted scoring and controlled workflow outcomes. Have them
   state which claims they verified, which they could not verify, and why.
8. A responsible human approves any public validation claim against the predeclared
   criteria, authenticity checks, conflicts and completed report. Publish exact
   scope, versions, dates and limitations with permission. There is currently no
   qualifying completed review, so the badge and broad validation claim remain
   disabled. Future qualifying evidence requires an explicit governed change;
   the present importer cannot enable a badge automatically.

Suggested review record fields: review ID; coordinator; protocol hash/date;
reviewer identity verification reference; declared conflicts; packet/dataset
hashes; evaluator/policy/suite versions; distribution and submission times;
original submission hashes; analysis hash; adjudication references; acceptance
criteria and decision; publication permission; corrections. Keep personal
identity evidence restricted rather than committing it to the repository.

Challenges can be tracked as repository issues when the submitter is authorised
to disclose the content. Sensitive security or customer evidence needs an agreed
private route. Establish that route before soliciting private submissions; this
document does not invent a staffed contact address or review organisation.
